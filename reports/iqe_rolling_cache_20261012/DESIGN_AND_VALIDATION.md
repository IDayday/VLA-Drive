## Bounded residency and scheduling

Session `pool46534/SESSION.json` binds the real46534 training checkpoint,
90,892 fit scenes in original order, original epoch16/offset1078 and100k
scheduler. Windows are16 optimizer updates/512 observations. Three windows
(current plus two upcoming) are requested; one prior window is retained.
The160GiB limit includes current generations and temporary files. Checkpoint
space is reserved separately. Existing44,632 permanently cached observations
are read-only references, never copied or garbage-collected.

`Pool.desired()` follows the slowest rank's preparation boundary, whereas every
checkpoint stores the actual consumed training cursor. Preparation is explicitly
not a resume cursor. Readers deliver original rank order including epoch tails.
Builders only work on requested windows; complete high-water windows cause them
to idle. They never sort/reorder the training sampler for IO locality.

Each producer leases stable shards and can steal unclaimed shards. Shards hold
three original visual microbatches (12 records, approximately649MiB), capped at
1GiB each. Sixteen writers therefore fit the original16GiB temporary reserve.
Each active producer is capped at96MiB/s: aggregate ceiling1.5GiB/s, not a measured
rate. The initial64MiB/s ceiling and its replacement are recorded below.
The visual tower and processor are resident on producer hosts; there is no
optimizer, backward, LLM or GT-based visual input there.

Storage computes complete-shard and per-record SHA256 while writing, fsyncs,
and commits atomically. Window publication checks receipt/intent agreement,
identity, byte coverage and exact key coverage. Readers verify every accessed
record, not entire multi-GB shards at every step. Interrupted final rename uses
full-shard SHA256 recovery before promoting an intent. Missing records wait
(up to900s) and then fail all ranks together; there is no online or zero fallback.

GC uses exclusive build/read leases and only removes its own generated bytes.
Readers close owned-shard descriptors before releasing the read lease, avoiding
deleted-but-open capacity leakage. An EVICTING intent is never readable and is
completed even after consumer rewind, then rebuilt in a new generation. Stale
incomplete generations are journalled and reclaimed under the same leases;
metadata/evidence survive. Replanning a stale producer request is a normal
SKIPPED_REPLAN outcome, not a worker failure.
