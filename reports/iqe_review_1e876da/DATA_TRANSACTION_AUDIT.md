# Round data transaction and leakage audit

`RoundDataTransaction` binds previous accepted weights/pool hash, immutable old scene snapshot, incoming manifest content, workflow config/steps and actual previous-serving snapshot. New scenes go to `round_k/data_staging/`; each old-expert/new-scene candidate/score is versioned there. Previous `scored.json` is never overwritten. Targets, buckets, fixed target IDs, sampling plan, feature references and hashes must exist before `DATA_COMMIT.json`. Only committed admissions become later input. `DATA_ADMISSION.json` is reconstructible after a crash.

Fault injection covers: after new-data audit; after feature cache; during old-pool-on-new scoring; after staged admission; before expert manifest; after commit/before global pointer. Every resumed result matches uninterrupted target IDs, bucket membership, sampling entries and candidate labels. The old bank checksum remains unchanged and admission count remains1. Same-path manifest changes raise stale; legacy half-admitted records remain on disk and produce LEGACY_ADMISSION_REPAIR instructions rather than being deleted. Fixtures are explicitly SYNTHETIC_UNIT_ONLY and never counted as real new data.

Actual diagnostic population: **44 observations**, incremental_fit32/30 groups, stage_val4/4 groups, selector_cal4/4 groups, dev_report4/1 group. Original role/group isolation is preserved. No new real or verified synthetic observations were supplied: all engineering rounds are **resampling_only**. E3 real admission remains UNTESTED. This does not block E2 once its Base quality gate is satisfied.

The real three-workflow sequence committed and subsequently checksum-reused every stage. Stage receipts hash external manifest contents, previous bank, actual checkpoint dependencies, DATA_COMMIT and relevant selector/threshold outputs. Full official Base training uses its original fit90892, val5563, cal5137, dev1696 population; the diagnostic cannot claim that scale or generalized improvement.

User-authorized navtest checkpoint monitoring now uses a **different NavtestInput schema with no target fields**, separate dataset/output root and access record. It cannot be parsed as a training SceneRecord. Full navtest12146/136 was checked for token/log disjointness from both actual train and dev indices. No navtest data or scoring records are admitted to expert/Scorer/Router fitting, retrieval or calibration.
