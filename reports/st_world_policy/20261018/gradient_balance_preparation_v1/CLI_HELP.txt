usage: audit_flow_gradient_balance.py [-h] --registration REGISTRATION
                                      [--execute]

Read-only four-rank C1 BC/FPO gradient decomposition at a saved boundary. No
optimizer, new scoring, model save, changed reward or changed BC definition. A
single allocated GPU sequentially reconstructs four original rank batches.
BF16 per-microbatch execution gradients are accumulated in FP32 on CPU: this
is an objective diagnostic, NOT a bit-exact reproduction of joint ZeRO2
backward. Only --execute consumes models, original train images/GT and saved
feedback.

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --registration REGISTRATION
                        Immutable Lead registration binding source, native
                        boundary, four receipts and resource limits
  --execute             Read real images/GT/models and run bounded gradient
                        diagnostic; never train or score
