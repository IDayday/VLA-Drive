usage: diagnose_road_direction.py [-h] --mode {prepare} [--evidence EVIDENCE]
                                  --output OUTPUT

Prepare the fixed 32-TRAIN road direction diagnostic for independent review.
This entry is intentionally preparation-only: it reads the already committed
small evidence document, never a rollout, metric cache, model, or score
service. Real execution requires a separate Lead admission and independently
reviewed invocation. Geometry APIs are in flow_posttraining.road_margin_proxy.

options:
  -h, --help           show this help message and exit
  --mode {prepare}
  --evidence EVIDENCE
  --output OUTPUT      New immutable JSON path, at most 2MiB
