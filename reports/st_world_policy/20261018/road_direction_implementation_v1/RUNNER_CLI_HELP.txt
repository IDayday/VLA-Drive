usage: run_road_direction.py [-h] (--prepare | --execute)
                             [--evidence EVIDENCE] --registration REGISTRATION
                             [--output-root OUTPUT_ROOT]
                             [--admission ADMISSION]
                             [--admission-sha256 ADMISSION_SHA256] [--resume]

Fixed32 CPU road-direction diagnostic. No model load, training, or retry.
Prepare emits a reviewable immutable registration. Execute requires an exact
Lead admission plus independently authored correctness/resource approvals
bound to that registration and these source hashes. Existing calls are never
repeated.

options:
  -h, --help            show this help message and exit
  --prepare
  --execute
  --evidence EVIDENCE
  --registration REGISTRATION
  --output-root OUTPUT_ROOT
                        Required by prepare; cannot change at execute
  --admission ADMISSION
  --admission-sha256 ADMISSION_SHA256
  --resume              Continue untouched scenes only, never retry an
                        admitted/partial scene
